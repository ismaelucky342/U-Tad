### **Course Overview**

The *Data Engineering Projects* course is oriented toward the development of real projects in the field of Data Engineering. It allows students to consolidate and reinforce the competencies acquired in the rest of the subjects, and to develop research, planning, teamwork, and professional skills, as well as software development, documentation, auditing, and public presentation in the context of Data Engineering.

In order not to restrict the acquisition and application of knowledge to a particular area of Data Engineering, students must not only develop a specific project but also participate in the audit of the projects assigned to other students/teams. This allows students to put into practice and consolidate the knowledge acquired in the different subjects of the Data Engineering minor. The course is also oriented toward favoring the student's subsequent integration into the labor market, as students participate in the development of projects from their definition to their subsequent deployment.

### **Learning Outcomes**

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

- Understand and implement efficient storage and administration methods in distributed, unstructured data environments.
- Know and apply the different supervised, semi-supervised, and unsupervised learning techniques.
- Understand and apply deep learning techniques.
- Design, implement, and deploy a complete data-based service or application, preferably using large volumes of data or heterogeneous sources.
- Apply team software development methodologies and monitor and control the software life cycle.
- Document, audit, and present a data engineering project in public.

### **Course Units**

1. **Unit 1: Analysis and Definition of Projects**
    - Working framework and roadmap, detailed project presentation, needs and functionalities analysis, user stories, and market study.
2. **Unit 2: System Design and Architecture**
    - Design and architecture of distributed systems and data-based applications, including the selection of technologies and data sources.
3. **Unit 3: Implementation, Data Processing and Modeling**
    - Data analysis, management, and visualization, data mining and machine learning, and team software development methodologies.
4. **Unit 4: Deployment, Audit and Presentation**
    - Deployment and service start-up, follow-up and control of the software life cycle, auditing of other teams' projects, and public presentation and defense.
