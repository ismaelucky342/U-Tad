### **Course Overview**

In this course we will learn computer vision techniques, both traditional and based on artificial neural networks. Starting from the fundamentals of the digital image, low-level image coding, and image processing, we will then move into deep learning-based vision using convolutional networks and their variations. During the semester, we will build different systems related to object recognition, autonomous vehicles, and image processing.

### **Learning Outcomes**

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

- Understand the nature and representation of digital images.
- Apply traditional image processing techniques, filtering, and feature detection.
- Understand and apply deep learning techniques.
- Know the applications of neural networks to the analysis and generation of sound, still images, and video.
- Develop software solutions applied to computer vision.

### **Course Units**

1. **Unit 1: Fundamentals of Digital Imaging**
    - What vision is and how digital image capture occurs, image representation, and electromagnetic spectrum basics.
2. **Unit 2: Basic Image Processing**
    - Convolution fundamentals, linear low-pass and high-pass filtering, non-linear filtering, Canny edge detection, feature detection (FAST, SIFT, SURF, BRIEF), and image invariances.
3. **Unit 3: Fundamentals of Convolutional Neural Networks**
    - A review of neural network fundamentals, the flatten and convolutional layers, binary and multi-class classification with CNNs, and data augmentation for images.
4. **Unit 4: Advanced Convolutional Networks**
    - Advanced architectures (VGG, ResNet, AlexNet), networks for segmentation and object detection (YOLO, Faster R-CNN), and transfer learning for images.
