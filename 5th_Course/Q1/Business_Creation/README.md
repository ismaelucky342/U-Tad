### **Course Overview**

This course provides true theoretical and practical training on the launch and start-up of business projects in the digital field. It offers interdisciplinary knowledge oriented toward approaching professional reality and its relationship with society, going beyond what is strictly necessary for the design of software or interactive systems. It brings a general business vision that is highly valuable in today's society, where self-employment is one of the most important professional outlets. Students learn about the company and its environment, the figure of the entrepreneur, the entrepreneurial process, the business plan, and the legal, fiscal, and financing aspects involved in setting up a company.

### **Learning Outcomes**

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

- Know the legal forms for establishing companies under Spanish legislation.
- Design the organization of the company with the aim of maximizing the team's contribution.
- Relate intellectual property legislation to various scenarios (national, European, and international).
- Identify relevant sources of economic information and their content.
- Know various marketing techniques and their implications for the development of a digital product.
- Reflect on the ethical and legal limits of technological innovations.
- Interpret relevant economic, political, and cultural data in software design.

### **Course Units**

1. **Unit 1: The Company, the Entrepreneur and Entrepreneurship**
    - The company as an economic agent, the figure of the entrepreneur and professional ethics, and the entrepreneurial process, business ideas, and differentiating factors.
2. **Unit 2: Creation and Organization of Business Projects**
    - The business plan, the production, purchasing, and organization plan, the marketing plan, and the economic-financial plan.
3. **Unit 3: Legal, Fiscal and Administrative Aspects**
    - Incorporation of the company, legal authorizations for opening a business, and regional, municipal, fiscal, and labor procedures.
4. **Unit 4: Information Search, Financing Sources and Entrepreneurship Support**
    - Sources of support for entrepreneurship (national, regional, and local administration, professional associations, universities), banking products, and other forms of financing.
